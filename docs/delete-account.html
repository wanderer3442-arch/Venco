Gym at Home

Account Deletion

Effective October 6, 2026  ·  Applies to the Gym at Home mobile app

01 Delete inside the app

You can delete your account at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Scroll down to the Delete Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account…, type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

The deletion takes effect immediately — no confirmation email or waiting period.

02 What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your login credentials and all data associated with your profile: workouts, meal logs, health metrics, water and sleep logs, habits, badges, preferences and settings. Nothing is kept in a queue and nothing can be recovered afterwards.

03 Your data is on your device only

Gym at Home stores everything locally. There is no server-side copy of your account, so no remote request is needed — deleting inside the app erases the account completely. As an alternative, you can also clear the app's data (Android Settings › Apps › Gym at Home › Storage › Clear data) or simply uninstall the app, which removes your account and data the same way.

04 Before you delete

If you want a copy of your data first, use Export & Reports in the app to download your plan and logs as a PDF or CSV. Exports are no longer possible after deletion.

05 Contact

Questions about account deletion or your data? Email gymathome.app@gmail.com.
