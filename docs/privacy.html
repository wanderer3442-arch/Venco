🛡 Privacy Policy

Gym at Home

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to the Gym at Home mobile app.

💾 Your data stays on your device

Gym at Home stores everything locally — your account, workouts, meals, health metrics and preferences. There is no user database on our servers, and app backups to the cloud are disabled. Removing the app removes your data.

🙈 No tracking, no ads, no selling

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your personal information. Your password is stored only on your device, hashed with PBKDF2.

✨ AI features (Google Gemini)

When you chat with the AI assistant, ask for photo-based food recognition or request a plan, the text (and any photo you choose to attach) is sent through our proxy to Google Gemini to generate a reply. That prompt may include health details you type. We do not store these conversations, and Google processes the request to generate the response.

🛡 What we can see

We have no access to your account, your logs or your files. We cannot recover your data for you, so export reports matter if you want a backup.

✉ Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email gymathome@gmail.com.
